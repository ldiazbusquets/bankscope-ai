HOW TO INTERPRET THE MODEL
01Liquidity

Uninsured deposit runoff is compared with cash and balances alone. The resulting shortfall is a cash-only gap, not a definitive liquidity shortfall.

02Capital

Illustrative credit losses reduce assets and equity equally. The model reports equity/assets, not regulatory CET1 or risk-based capital under stress.

03Interpretation

Scenarios explore sensitivity under chosen assumptions. They cannot determine the probability of bank failure or predict a real bank run.

04 / RESEARCH TRANSPARENCY

Methods & data provenance

REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN
01 · DATA INGESTION

FDIC BankFind API

Search FDIC-insured bank charters, pull quarterly financial records by FDIC certificate, sort by reporting date, and preserve nulls for missing values. The Python workflow retrieves up to 12 quarters for each configured bank. The chart displays eight.

Official FDIC API documentation ↗
02 · QUANTITATIVE ENGINE

Calculated in Python

Equity/assets, loans/deposits, cash/deposits, and uninsured domestic deposit share are computed deterministically. Published ratios are shown directly where available. Stress results are explicit accounting simplifications.

Financial report reference ↗
03 · AI LAYER

Evidence-constrained commentary

The AI endpoint receives an explicit snapshot, field definitions, flags, and stress assumptions. The GitHub Actions build can send structured financial evidence to a configured LLM through a private Actions secret, then publish an attributed AI report. The public site does not expose keys. Without a model, deterministic analysis works.

04 · IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Screening, not supervision

Bank-level data must not be conflated with consolidated holding-company results. The thresholds are research heuristics, not legal capital criteria. Market risk, off-balance-sheet exposures, and management responses are not fully modeled.
