What this model leaves out

Securities that could be sold or pledged, Federal Home Loan Bank and Federal Reserve borrowing, foreign-office deposits, taxes, risk-weighted assets, and anything management would do in response. A cash-only gap is therefore a sensitivity measure, not a liquidity shortfall, and this is not a supervisory stress test.

How the data gets here

  1. Collect. A scheduled GitHub Actions job queries the FDIC BankFind /financials endpoint for up to twelve quarters per institution.
  2. Calculate. Python computes derived ratios, screening checks and the baseline stress case. Missing fields stay blank rather than being filled with zero.
  3. Publish. The results are written as JSON and deployed to GitHub Pages with the site. The browser recalculates only the interactive stress scenarios.

Reading the results

Each figure belongs to an individual FDIC-insured bank charter. JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. is not the same reporting entity as JPMorgan Chase & Co., its holding company.

Dollar amounts are reported to the FDIC in thousands and displayed here in millions, billions or trillions. Return on assets, return on equity and net interest margin are year-to-date figures annualized by the FDIC.

Equity / assets is an accounting measure. It is related to, but different from, the regulatory Tier 1 leverage and risk-based capital ratios.
